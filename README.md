# Academic-Chatbot
Academic AI: Your Notes, But Smarter.
The multimodal study companion that gives your notebooks a voice.

Introduction
Let’s face it: Studying is hard. Transcribing handwritten lecture notes or trying to copy-paste text from a locked PDF is a waste of your brainpower.

Academic AI was built to solve that. It’s a clean, distraction-free "Purple Space" where you can upload a photo of your messy handwriting or a complex diagram, and instantly start a conversation with it. It doesn’t just "read" your notes—it understands them.

Features
Vision-Ready: Powered by Qwen 2.5 VL, it can interpret diagrams, charts, and that one line of handwriting you can’t even read yourself.
PDF Magic: No more copy-pasting. Drop a PDF, and the AI "looks" at the page to explain it to you.
Calm UI: A minimalist purple aesthetic designed to keep you focused and reduce late-night eye strain.
Deep Memory: It remembers the context of your conversation. Ask a question, then ask a follow-up—it stays on track.
Frictionless: No accounts, no "confirm your email" loops. Just open, upload, and learn.
The Tech Stack (Under the Hood)
Backend: FastAPI (The speed demon of Python frameworks).
PDF Engine: PyMuPDF (Blazing fast PDF-to-image conversion).
AI Orchestration: OpenRouter API (Connecting to SOTA Vision-Language Models).
Frontend: HTML & CSS (Zero-dependency, lightweight, and blazing fast).

How to Use
The Prompt: Type your question in the box (e.g., "Explain the second paragraph").
The + Button: Click the purple plus to attach your photo or PDF.
The Result: Get instant, professional academic feedback.

Learning Outcomes
This project was a deep dive into:
Multimodal AI: Learning how models "see" images vs. how they "read" text.
Stream Processing: Handling files in-memory to keep things fast and private.
UX Design: Using color theory and minimalism to build a tool people actually want to use.
